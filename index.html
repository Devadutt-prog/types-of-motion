What is Motion?

Motion is the change in position of an object with time. Everything around us, from moving cars to spinning fans, shows different types of motion. Let's explore them!

🚀 Types of Motion

Click on any card to discover more information!


🚗

1. Rectilinear Motion

Movement of an object along a straight line.

Example: A train moving on a straight railway track. Its path is straight.

Key feature: Straight path.

🎡

2. Circular Motion

Movement of an object along a circular path.

Example: A stone tied to a string and rotated in a circle.

Key feature: Circular path.

🌀

3. Rotational Motion

When an object spins around its own axis.

Example: A spinning ceiling fan or a rotating wheel.

Key feature: Spinning around an axis.

⏰

4. Oscillatory Motion

Repeated to-and-fro movement around a fixed position.

Example: A swinging pendulum.

Key feature: Back-and-forth movement.

🌍

5. Periodic Motion

Motion that repeats itself after equal intervals of time.

Example: The Earth revolving around the Sun.

Key feature: Repeats at regular time intervals.

🚦

6. Non-Periodic Motion

Motion that does not repeat itself at regular intervals of time.

Example: A car moving through busy traffic, stopping and starting irregularly.

Key feature: No regular repetition.
